TextServices Demo

Tools for building and exploring IIIF text services — search indexes, annotation pages, and augmented manifests.

Builder

Submit IIIF manifests or page sequences to build text indexes. Monitor job progress and access derived outputs.

Viewer

Load a text-augmented manifest and search it. Highlights matched words on image canvases and seeks to matched cues on video canvases.

Search Comparator

Compare autocomplete and search results from multiple IIIF Content Search services side by side.

Figures

Browse figures, tables and illustrations identified from ALTO ComposedBlock elements, with image crop thumbnails.

Annotation Comparator

Compare source annotation pages from a manifest with the generated line- and word-level transcription pages side by side.
